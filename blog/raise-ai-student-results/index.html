How do you know if an AI course actually works?

You look at what its students made after the course. Every institute says it is the best. Only results can be checked.

A good AI course leaves you with finished work you can use in your job or sell to a client. So the fair test is simple: can the institute show you its students, their work and their own words? If it can only show a certificate, a syllabus or a famous tool logo, you are being sold a promise.

How do you check any AI institute in Kerala?

Use these questions before you pay anyone, including us. Copy the prompt to compare two or three courses side by side.

The questions that matter most:

  • Can I see work made by your students, not by the trainer?
  • Can I see or hear reviews from named students?
  • What will I have finished by the last class?
  • Is it live, and do I get the recordings?
  • Is it taught in a language I am comfortable with?
  • What is the refund policy if it is not what you described?
