How to make an AI video ad for free (step by step)

By Hadhi Muhammed P. · 12 Oct 2026 · Updated 13 Oct 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: Write a 30-second script in ChatGPT, turn each line into a short video clip in Google Flow or Luma Dream Machine, make the voiceover in ElevenLabs, and put it all together in CapCut. You do not need a camera, a studio or paid software to make your first ad. Free plans and their limits change often, so check each tool's current plan before you start.

What free tools do you need?

You do not need a cinema camera, lights or a studio shoot to make an advertising video. With the right AI workflow, these free tools cover every step:

  • ChatGPT (free plan): an AI chat assistant. Use it for hook ideas, the script and the shot list for a 30-second video.
  • Google Flow or Luma Dream Machine: AI video generators. They turn a written description (a prompt) into a short video clip.
  • ElevenLabs (free plan): an AI voice tool. It reads your script aloud in a natural human voice.
  • CapCut (free): a video editor. Use it to put the clips together, add captions and add background music.

One thing to check first: some free plans add a watermark to what you make, or do not allow commercial use. Read each tool's current terms before you use a free-plan video in a paid ad.

Step 1: Write a hook and a 30-second script

Every ad video succeeds or fails in its first 3 seconds. When people scroll through Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts, your hook has to stop their thumb.

Use this four-part structure for a 30-second ad:

  1. The hook (0 to 3 seconds): Call out the person the ad is for, or name a problem they did not expect. Example: "Still spending 3 hours filming product videos every week?"
  2. The pain point (3 to 10 seconds): Show the frustration: expensive cameras, retakes and slow editing.
  3. The solution (10 to 22 seconds): Introduce the product or the change it brings, with clear visuals.
  4. The call to action (22 to 30 seconds): Tell viewers exactly what to do next. Example: "Tap the link below to get yours today."

Step 2: Generate the video scenes

Once your script is ready, break it into 4 to 6 separate shots. Each shot covers 4 to 5 seconds of the video.

When you prompt an AI video tool like Luma Dream Machine or Google Flow, use the Subject + Action + Camera + Lighting formula:

Prompt formula: "Close-up cinematic shot of [Product Name] resting on a polished marble surface, soft morning sunlight casting realistic shadows, smooth slow camera push-in, 4k commercial lighting."

Step 3: Generate the voiceover

A flat, robotic voice makes an ad feel cheap. In ElevenLabs, pick a voice from the voice library that suits a commercial, paste your script and generate the audio. The free plan has a monthly limit on how much text you can turn into speech.

Tip: add commas and ellipses (...) inside your script to create natural pauses and a conversational rhythm.

Step 4: Edit and caption the video in CapCut

Import your AI video clips and the ElevenLabs audio into CapCut. Then:

  • Cut tight: trim each clip to the voiceover. Keep cuts fast, every 2 to 3 seconds.
  • Add captions: many people watch ads with the sound off. Use CapCut's auto captions and set them in a bold, high-contrast font.
  • Add sound: put a soft whoosh on transitions and a royalty-free background track at about 15% volume.

Questions people ask

Can I really make an AI video ad completely for free?

Yes, for your first ad. The free plans of ChatGPT, Google Flow or Luma Dream Machine, ElevenLabs and CapCut are enough to make a 30-second ad. Some free plans add a watermark or do not allow commercial use, so check each tool's current terms before you run the ad.

Which free AI video generator is best for commercial ads?

This guide uses Google Flow and Luma Dream Machine. Both turn a text prompt into a short video clip. Try the same prompt in both and keep the one that looks better for your product.

How long does it take to make an AI video ad?

Once you understand the workflow, making and editing a 30-second ad takes about 45 to 60 minutes from prompt to export.
