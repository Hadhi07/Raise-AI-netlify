What should an AI course for architects teach?

Most general AI courses teach chatbots and theory. A design practice needs something narrower and more useful. These are the six skills that save real hours:

SkillWhat you can do after itFree guide to start
Prompting for project workBriefs, concept options, client emails, specificationsChatGPT prompts for architects
Sketch to renderA photoreal concept image from a sketch, plan or model screenshotSketch to render with AI
Room restyling and stagingDesign options from a site photo, furnished empty rooms, mood boardsGemini prompts for interior designers
Walkthrough videoA short cinematic film from approved stillsAI walkthrough video from a render
Site and BOQ paperworkDaily reports, BOQ checks, schedules, contractor lettersChatGPT prompts for civil engineers
Marketing your practiceProject reels and posts that bring enquiriesHow to make an AI video ad

If a course covers only one of these, such as image generation, it is a tool tutorial and not a course for your profession.

How do you choose an AI course?

Use this checklist before you pay for any course, including ours.

CheckGood signWarning sign
Live or recordedLive classes where you can ask questionsOnly old recordings
Your own workYou bring a real project and leave with outputEveryone copies the same demo
ProofThe teacher shows real work made with the methodOnly screenshots of tools
ToolsCurrent, mainstream tools you can keep usingTools you have never heard of, or one paid tool only
LanguageTaught in a language you are comfortable inFast English with heavy jargon
RecordingsYou get the recording of every classNo replay
After classA group or support channel for questionsNothing after the last session

Two more questions are worth asking. First, does the course admit what AI cannot do? A trainer who says AI will produce your working drawings or structural design is selling, not teaching. Second, is it built for your field? An architect, an interior designer and a site engineer use the same tools for very different jobs.

Can you learn AI for architecture for free?

Yes, to a useful level. The tools have free plans, and the method is public.

  1. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and paste the project card from our prompts for architects.
  2. Take one live project and run three prompts on it this week: the design brief, the concept options and one client email.
  3. Upload one sketch and follow the sketch to render steps.
  4. Turn the best image into a clip with the walkthrough video guide.

Self-learning works for people who practise every week. A live course mainly buys you speed, feedback on your own work, and other professionals to learn with.

What can AI not do for an architect?

  • It does not produce measured, buildable drawings.
  • It does not do structural design or sign anything.
  • It does not know the current building rules unless you paste them in.
  • It can invent details in images and facts in text, so every output needs your check.

A course that is honest about these limits is worth more than one that promises magic.

Questions people ask

Which is the best AI course for architects in Kerala?

The best AI course for an architect is one that is live, built for design practice, and makes you produce real work on your own project. Raise AI in Kozhikode runs a live online cohort for architects, interior designers and civil engineers, taught in Malayalam with English terms. Use the 7-point checklist above to compare any option.

Do architects need coding to learn AI?

No. Everything in this guide is done by typing plain instructions into tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google Flow. If you can write a clear brief for a junior, you can prompt an AI.

Is the Raise AI architects cohort online or offline?

It is online and live on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere in Kerala, the rest of India or the Gulf. You get the recording after each session.

How long does it take to learn AI for architecture?

You can get useful results in the first week: a better brief, a render from a sketch, a client email. Being fast and consistent across all six skills usually takes a few weeks of practice on real projects.

Which AI tools should an architect learn first?

Start with one chat assistant, ChatGPT or Gemini, for writing and images. Add Google Flow when you want video. Learn more tools only when a real project needs them.

Will AI replace architects and interior designers?

No. Clients still need someone to understand the site, the budget, the rules and the family. AI takes over repeat work such as first drafts, options and visuals, which gives designers who use it more time for design.
