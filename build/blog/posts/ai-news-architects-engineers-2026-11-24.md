## What is ChatGPT Live Dictation, and how can site engineers use it?

ChatGPT now turns your speech into text **while you are still talking**, instead of after you stop. According to OpenAI's release notes of 18 November 2026, Live Dictation is rolling out globally to all ChatGPT plans on iOS, Android, the web and the desktop app, and the old 10-minute recording limit has been removed.

For people who spend the day on site, this is the most practical change of the week. You can walk the site, speak your observations into the phone, and see them written out before you pocket it. Pair it with a structured prompt and the notes become a report.

:::prompt Turn dictated site notes into a daily report | ChatGPT
I am a site engineer. Below are notes I just dictated on site, in English or Malayalam. Turn them into a daily progress report.

Sections: Work done today (by location), Labour on site, Materials received, Issues and delays (with the cause), Plan for tomorrow.

Use only what is in my notes. Write "not recorded" for anything missing. Keep it short.

My notes:
[DICTATE HERE]
:::

For more prompts like this, see our [ChatGPT prompts for civil engineers](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-civil-engineers/).

## What is Tasks in Gemini, and is it really free in India?

Yes. Tasks in Gemini is an AI assistant that works **in the background** on multi-step jobs and recurring routines, and notifies you when the result is ready. Google's release notes of 17 November 2026 say it is rolling out free of charge in the US, India, Canada, Australia, Japan and Korea. It was earlier launched in India as Gemini Spark for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

Google's help pages add the conditions: you must be 18 or older, use a personal Google account (not a work or school account), and usage limits depend on how many tasks you set and how complex they are.

For architects and engineers, the obvious use is a morning briefing built from your own calendar and email, so you know which site visit and which client call come first.

:::prompt Set up a daily project briefing | Gemini
Every weekday at 7:30 AM, send me a short briefing:

1. Today's meetings and site visits from my calendar, in time order, with the location.
2. Emails from the last 24 hours that need a reply from me, grouped by project.
3. Anything with a deadline in the next 3 days.

Keep it under 150 words. Use project names, not email subjects.
:::

Only connect accounts you are allowed to use with Gemini, and check the briefing before acting on it.

## What can Gemini Notebook's "make me a site" do for a design project?

Gemini Notebook (the new name for NotebookLM) can now build an **interactive mini website from your notebook's sources**. You type "make me a site" in the notebook chat with a description of what you want, and you can open the result in a new tab or share it. Google's help pages say it is rolling out on the web for users aged 18 and over, including Google Workspace subscribers, with the mobile app coming soon. Creating a site needs edit access to the notebook, and public sharing needs a personal account with owner or editor rights.

For a design practice, the sources can be your project brief, meeting minutes and specification notes. The site becomes a simple project page for the client: stages, decisions still pending and the next steps.

:::prompt Make a client project page from your notes | Gemini Notebook
make me a site: a simple project page for my client, built only from the sources in this notebook.

Include:
- The project in 3 lines (type, location, size, stage)
- A timeline of stages, marking which are done
- Decisions the client still needs to make, with the date needed
- Next steps for the coming 2 weeks

Clean and simple design, mobile friendly, no prices unless they are in the sources.
:::

Do not add drawings or client details to a notebook you plan to share publicly unless the client has agreed.

## What are GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, and do architects need them?

On 17 November 2026 OpenAI released two new models: **GPT-6.2 Astra**, its most capable model, and **GPT-6.2 Sol**, a faster and cheaper everyday model. They are available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, not in regular chat and not on the free plan. OpenAI says the difference shows in slideshows, websites and 3D designs, and that the models can turn a written brief into an interactive browser experience.

For architects already paying for ChatGPT, the useful part is better presentations and documents from a brief. If you are on the free plan, nothing changes this week; the free prompts in our [ChatGPT prompts for architects](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-architects/) still work.

:::prompt Turn a design brief into a client presentation | ChatGPT
Make a 10-slide client presentation from the design brief below.

Slide plan: the client's needs, the site, 3 concept directions with one line each, the recommended direction, zoning, materials, next steps and the decision I need today.

Short titles, at most 3 points per slide, simple words. Leave a clear space for one image on each slide and tell me which image to place there.

Design brief:
[PASTE YOUR BRIEF]
:::
