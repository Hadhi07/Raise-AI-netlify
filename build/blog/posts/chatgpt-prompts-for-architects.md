:::takeaways
- A good architecture prompt has four parts: role, project facts, limits and output format.
- ChatGPT and Gemini are strong at briefs, options, emails, specifications and checklists. They are weak at exact dimensions and scaled drawings.
- Save your project facts once as a "project card" and paste it at the top of every chat.
- For building rules, paste the clause into the chat. Never ask the AI to recall a rule from memory.
- You stay responsible for the design. Treat every answer as a draft from a junior, and check it.
:::

## What makes a ChatGPT prompt work for architecture?

Most weak answers come from weak prompts. "Design a 3 bedroom house" gives you a generic essay. A prompt that works gives the AI the same things you would give a junior architect on day one:

| Part | What to write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Who the AI should act as | "Act as a senior residential architect in Kerala" |
| Project facts | Plot, area, client, budget, climate | "5 cent plot, 1,800 sq ft, family of 5, warm-humid climate" |
| Limits | What it must not do or assume | "Do not invent setbacks. Ask me if a fact is missing" |
| Output format | How you want the answer | "A table with 3 options and one line on each" |

The fastest way to do this is to write your project facts once and reuse them. Start every chat with this card.

:::prompt 1. Project card (paste this first in every chat) | ChatGPT, Gemini
You are helping me, a practising architect in [CITY, STATE], on one project. Use these facts in every answer. If something you need is missing, ask me. Do not guess.

Project: [HOUSE / APARTMENT / OFFICE / CLINIC / OTHER]
Client: [WHO THEY ARE, FAMILY SIZE OR BUSINESS]
Plot: [AREA IN CENTS OR SQ M], [SHAPE], road on the [SIDE], [SLOPE OR FLAT]
Built-up area: [SQ FT]
Floors: [G / G+1 / G+2]
Budget: [RS RANGE]
Style the client likes: [WORDS OR REFERENCES]
Climate: [WARM-HUMID / HOT-DRY / OTHER], heavy rain from [MONTHS]
Must have: [LIST]
Must avoid: [LIST]

Reply with "Project card saved" and one question you still need answered.
:::

## Which prompts help with the brief and the concept?

These three prompts turn a messy first meeting into something you can design from.

:::prompt 2. Client notes to design brief | ChatGPT, Gemini
Below are my rough notes from the first client meeting. Turn them into a one-page design brief.

Use these headings: Client and lifestyle, Rooms and sizes they asked for, Budget and timeline, Style and references, Site facts, Open questions.

Under "Open questions", list everything the client has not decided yet, as questions I can send them on WhatsApp.

Do not add requirements the client did not mention.

My notes:
[PASTE NOTES OR A VOICE NOTE TRANSCRIPT]
:::

:::prompt 3. Three concept directions | ChatGPT, Gemini
Using the project card, give me 3 different concept directions for this project.

For each one give:
- A short name
- The main idea in 2 sentences
- How it handles sun, rain and cross ventilation
- Zoning: which rooms face which side, and why
- One risk or cost I should know

Put the 3 options in a comparison table at the end. Keep it practical for the budget. No dimensions, I will do those.
:::

:::prompt 4. Site visit checklist | ChatGPT, Gemini
I am visiting the site for this project tomorrow. Make a site visit checklist I can tick on my phone.

Group it under: Access and road, Boundaries and neighbours, Levels and drainage, Trees and existing structures, Sun, wind and views, Services (water, power, sewage), Soil and water table, Photos to take.

Keep each item to one short line. Add 5 questions to ask the owner on site.
:::

## Which prompts save time on client communication?

Emails, fee notes and meeting minutes eat hours every week. These prompts give you a clean first draft in your own tone.

:::prompt 5. Fee proposal email | ChatGPT, Gemini
Write a short, polite email to a client with my fee proposal.

Project: [PROJECT]
Scope: [CONCEPT / WORKING DRAWINGS / 3D VIEWS / SITE VISITS / OTHER]
Fee: [AMOUNT OR PERCENTAGE]
Stages and payment at each stage: [LIST]
Not included: [LIST, FOR EXAMPLE STRUCTURAL DESIGN, APPROVAL FEES]
Timeline: [WEEKS PER STAGE]

Tone: warm and professional, plain English, no jargon. Under 180 words. End with one clear next step.
:::

:::prompt 6. Explain a design decision to a client | ChatGPT, Gemini
My client is asking why I did this: [THE DECISION, FOR EXAMPLE "moved the kitchen to the east side"].

My real reasons: [YOUR REASONS IN ROUGH WORDS].

Write a WhatsApp message that explains it in simple words a non-architect will understand. Use one everyday comparison. Under 90 words. Do not sound defensive.
:::

:::prompt 7. Meeting minutes and action list | ChatGPT, Gemini
Here is the transcript or my notes from a project meeting. Write minutes I can send to everyone.

Format:
1. Decisions made (bullets)
2. Changes to the drawings (bullets, with the room or drawing name)
3. Action list as a table: task, who, by when
4. Points still open

Only use what is in the notes. If a date or owner is missing, write "to confirm".

Notes:
[PASTE]
:::

## Which prompts help with drawings and specifications?

ChatGPT cannot draw your working drawings, but it is a good checker and a fast writer of the text around them.

:::prompt 8. Drawing set checklist | ChatGPT, Gemini
I am preparing the working drawing set for this project. List every drawing a contractor will need, grouped as Architectural, Structural (by the structural engineer), Electrical, Plumbing and Finishes.

For each drawing give one line on what it must show. Mark the 5 drawings that most often cause site mistakes when they are missing or unclear.
:::

:::prompt 9. Outline specification | ChatGPT, Gemini
Write an outline specification for this project that I can edit.

Cover: foundation, structure, walls, plaster, flooring by room, doors and windows, roofing and waterproofing, painting, electrical, plumbing and sanitary.

For each item give: a mid-range option and a premium option, both commonly available in India. Use a table. Where a choice depends on the structural engineer, write "as per structural design". Do not give prices.
:::

:::prompt 10. Check a rule clause (paste the clause) | ChatGPT, Gemini
I will paste a clause from the building rules that apply to my site. Do not use your own memory of the rules. Use only the pasted text.

1. Explain the clause in simple words.
2. List the numbers in it (setbacks, coverage, height, parking) as a table.
3. Apply it to my project using the project card, and show your working.
4. List anything that is unclear and that I should confirm with the local body.

Clause:
[PASTE THE EXACT TEXT FROM KMBR, KPBR OR YOUR LOCAL RULES]
:::

:::note Why paste the clause?
AI chatbots often get building rules wrong when they answer from memory, and rules change. Pasting the exact text from your current rule book, such as KMBR or KPBR in Kerala, keeps the answer tied to the real wording. You still confirm it with the local body.
:::

## Which prompts help with renders and presentations?

Both ChatGPT and Gemini can turn a sketch or a model screenshot into a realistic image. The full method is in our guide on [how to turn a sketch into a photoreal render with AI](/blog/sketch-to-render-ai/). These two prompts get you started.

:::prompt 11. Write my render prompt | ChatGPT, Gemini
I will upload a sketch or a 3D model screenshot. Write me an image prompt to turn it into a photoreal render.

The prompt must:
- Keep the exact form, openings and proportions of my image
- Describe materials: [YOUR MATERIALS, FOR EXAMPLE laterite, exposed concrete, teak, clay tile]
- Set the light: [MORNING / NOON / DUSK / MONSOON SKY]
- Set the camera: [EYE LEVEL / AERIAL / INTERIOR WIDE]
- Add context: [TROPICAL PLANTING, NEIGHBOURING HOUSES, ROAD]

Give me one main prompt and 2 variations with a different mood.
:::

:::prompt 12. Client presentation story | ChatGPT, Gemini
I am presenting this design to the client in a 15-minute meeting. Build the story for me.

Give me 8 slides. For each slide: a title of 6 words or fewer, the one image I should show, and 2 lines I should say.

Start with their problem, not my design. End with the decision I need from them today: [THE DECISION].
:::

When the stills are approved, you can turn them into a short film for the client. See [how to make an AI walkthrough video from a render](/blog/ai-walkthrough-video-from-render/).

## What should architects not use ChatGPT for?

- **Scaled drawings and dimensions.** Image tools make pictures, not measured plans. A floor plan image from AI will not hold its dimensions.
- **Structural design.** Sizes of beams, columns and footings come from your structural engineer.
- **Rule compliance from memory.** Always paste the clause and confirm with the local body.
- **Client data you promised to keep private.** Remove names, addresses and survey numbers before you paste.

Interior designers will find matching prompts in [Gemini prompts for interior designers](/blog/gemini-prompts-for-interior-designers/), and site engineers in [ChatGPT prompts for civil engineers](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-civil-engineers/).

:::faq
### Can ChatGPT design a house plan?
ChatGPT can suggest room layouts, zoning ideas and concept options in words, and it can draw a rough plan image. It cannot produce a measured, buildable floor plan. Use it for options and briefs, then draw the plan yourself in your CAD or BIM software.

### Is ChatGPT or Gemini better for architects?
Both handle every prompt in this guide. For image work, Google describes Gemini's image model as built to change one part of an uploaded picture and leave the rest alone, which suits sketches and site photos. Try the same prompt in both and keep the better answer.

### Are these prompts free to use?
Yes. Copy them, change them and share them with your team. They work on the free plans of ChatGPT and Gemini, though free plans limit how many files and images you can use each day.

### Is it safe to paste client details into ChatGPT?
Remove anything that identifies the client or the plot, such as names, phone numbers, addresses and survey numbers, before you paste. Check your account's data settings, and follow any confidentiality terms in your agreement with the client.
