:::takeaways
- You need 4 to 6 good still renders. AI renders, V-Ray stills and Lumion stills all work.
- In Google Flow, use Frames to Video: your render is the first frame, and your prompt describes the camera move.
- One slow camera move per clip. Slow moves hide errors, fast moves create them.
- Always add a line telling the AI to keep the architecture exactly as in the image.
- A 30-second film is 5 or 6 short clips joined in CapCut with music. Plan about an hour for your first one.
:::

## What is an AI walkthrough video?

An AI walkthrough video starts from a still image, not a 3D camera path. You give an AI video tool one render, and it creates a few seconds of camera movement through that view: a slow push towards the entrance, a pan across the living room, curtains moving in the wind.

It is different from a rendered animation:

| | Traditional animation | AI walkthrough from stills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starts from | A full 3D model and camera path | One still image per shot |
| Time | Hours or days of rendering | Minutes per clip |
| Hardware | A strong PC or render farm | A browser |
| Accuracy | Exact to the model | Close, but the AI may invent details |
| Best for | Final marketing films, exact routes | Concept films, client mood, social media reels |

This is the same skill we teach for product ads at Raise AI, applied to buildings. The tool is the same and the prompts are simpler, because a building does not move.

## What do you need before you start?

1. **4 to 6 still images of the project.** Exterior hero view, entrance, living room, kitchen or dining, main bedroom, and one detail or garden view. If you only have sketches, first make stills with our [sketch to render guide](/blog/sketch-to-render-ai/).
2. **Google Flow.** Flow is Google's AI video tool. It runs in the browser. Google lists a free tier with daily credits, and paid Google AI plans add more credits each month.
3. **A video editor.** CapCut is free and enough for this.

:::note Check your images first
The AI animates whatever is in the image, including mistakes. Fix crooked verticals, odd furniture and wrong materials in the still before you make any video. Landscape images at 16:9 suit a client presentation. Vertical 9:16 suits Instagram Reels.
:::

## How do you turn a render into a video in Google Flow?

1. Open Google Flow and start a new project.
2. Choose **Frames to Video**. This mode lets you set the starting frame of the shot.
3. Upload your render as the first frame.
4. Write a prompt that describes only the camera move and small natural motion. Use the formula below.
5. Generate. Make 2 or 3 takes of each shot and keep the best one.
6. Optional: add a second image as the last frame, so the camera travels from one view to the other.
7. Download the clips.

The prompt formula for a walkthrough shot is **Camera move + Speed + What moves + What must stay the same**.

:::prompt 1. Exterior hero shot: slow push-in | Google Flow
Slow, smooth camera push-in towards the front entrance of the house. Steady, like a gimbal shot at walking speed.

Palm leaves move gently in the wind. Soft clouds drift. Light stays the same.

Keep the architecture exactly as in the image: same form, materials, windows and proportions. No people, no cars, no new objects. Photorealistic.
:::

:::prompt 2. Interior: slow pan across the room | Google Flow
Slow, smooth camera pan from left to right across the living room, at eye level. Steady tripod movement.

Sheer curtains move slightly. Sunlight on the floor stays steady.

Keep the room exactly as in the image: same furniture, layout, materials and colours. Do not add or remove anything. No people. Photorealistic interior film.
:::

:::prompt 3. Interior: walk forward through the space | Google Flow
The camera moves slowly forward through the room towards the [WINDOW / DINING AREA / DOORWAY], at eye level, smooth and steady, as if walking.

Keep the walls, furniture and layout exactly as in the image. Reveal only what would naturally be seen ahead. No people, no sudden changes. Photorealistic, natural daylight.
:::

## Which camera moves work best for architecture?

Slow and simple wins. Every extra instruction is another chance for the AI to bend a wall.

| Camera move | Use it for | Prompt words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Push-in | Exterior hero, entrance, feature wall | "slow push-in towards the entrance" |
| Pull-back | Revealing a room or the full facade | "slow pull-back to reveal the whole living room" |
| Pan | Wide interiors, long elevations | "slow pan left to right at eye level" |
| Rise (crane up) | Showing roof, site and surroundings | "camera rises slowly from eye level to above the roof" |
| Orbit | A corner view of the building | "slow orbit a quarter turn around the house" |
| Still with life | Details, pools, gardens | "camera stays still, water ripples, leaves move" |

:::prompt 4. Aerial rise over the site | Google Flow
The camera rises slowly and smoothly from eye level to a high view above the roof, tilting down slightly to show the house and its plot. Drone-style movement, steady.

Trees move gently. Keep the building, compound wall, driveway and landscape exactly as in the image. Do not invent neighbouring buildings. Photorealistic.
:::

:::prompt 5. Day to dusk in one shot | Google Flow
The camera stays almost still, with a very slow push-in. Over the shot, the light changes from late afternoon to dusk. Interior lights turn on warmly, and the sky deepens to blue.

Keep the architecture, materials and landscape exactly as in the image. No people. Photorealistic time-lapse feel.
:::

## How do you edit the clips into one film?

1. **Order the shots like a visit.** Outside, entrance, living, kitchen or dining, bedroom, detail, and back outside at dusk.
2. **Trim hard.** Keep the best 3 to 5 seconds of each clip. Cut before anything bends or flickers.
3. **Use simple cuts.** A plain cut or a short cross-dissolve looks more premium than flashy transitions.
4. **Add music at low volume.** Calm, instrumental, royalty-free.
5. **Add 3 or 4 text lines.** Project name, location, area in sq ft, and your studio name with a contact number.
6. **Export twice.** 16:9 for the client presentation and 9:16 for Instagram Reels and WhatsApp status.

For a reel that sells the project and not only shows it, borrow the hook and script structure from our guide on [how to make an AI video ad](/blog/how-to-make-an-ai-video-ad-for-free/).

## What goes wrong, and how do you fix it?

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Walls or windows bend during the move | Slow the move down, shorten the clip, and repeat the keep line |
| New furniture or people appear | Add "do not add anything, no people" and generate another take |
| The camera flies too fast | Write "very slow" and "walking speed", and name only one move |
| The clip does not match the next one | Use the last frame of clip 1 as the first frame of clip 2 |
| Colours shift between shots | Make all stills with the same light first, then animate |

Be honest with clients about what it is. An AI walkthrough is a mood film made from approved stills. It may add small details that are not in the design, so do not present it as the exact final building.

:::faq
### Can I make an architectural walkthrough video with AI for free?
You can start for free. Google lists a free tier for Flow with a daily credit allowance, and CapCut is free for editing. Longer or higher quality videos use more credits, which come with paid Google AI plans. Check Google's Flow page for the current numbers.

### Which AI tool is best for turning a render into a video?
This guide uses Google Flow because it lets you set your render as the first frame and describe the camera move in plain words. Other image-to-video tools work in a similar way. Test one exterior and one interior shot before you choose.

### How long can an AI walkthrough clip be?
Each generated clip is short: seconds, not minutes. A full walkthrough is made by joining several clips in a video editor. That is why you plan 4 to 6 shots and not one long take.

### Do I need a 3D model to make an AI walkthrough?
No. You need still images. They can come from rendering software, from an AI render made from a sketch, or even from a good photo of a finished project.

### Can interior designers and builders use this too?
Yes. Interior designers use it to show a room before work starts, and builders use it for project launch reels and WhatsApp marketing. The steps are the same.
