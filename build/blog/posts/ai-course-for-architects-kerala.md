## What should an AI course for architects teach?

Most general AI courses teach chatbots and theory. A design practice needs something narrower and more useful. These are the six skills that save real hours:

| Skill | What you can do after it | Free guide to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prompting for project work | Briefs, concept options, client emails, specifications | [ChatGPT prompts for architects](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-architects/) |
| Sketch to render | A photoreal concept image from a sketch, plan or model screenshot | [Sketch to render with AI](/blog/sketch-to-render-ai/) |
| Room restyling and staging | Design options from a site photo, furnished empty rooms, mood boards | [Gemini prompts for interior designers](/blog/gemini-prompts-for-interior-designers/) |
| Walkthrough video | A short cinematic film from approved stills | [AI walkthrough video from a render](/blog/ai-walkthrough-video-from-render/) |
| Site and BOQ paperwork | Daily reports, BOQ checks, schedules, contractor letters | [ChatGPT prompts for civil engineers](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-civil-engineers/) |
| Marketing your practice | Project reels and posts that bring enquiries | [How to make an AI video ad](/blog/how-to-make-an-ai-video-ad-for-free/) |

If a course covers only one of these, such as image generation, it is a tool tutorial and not a course for your profession.

## How do you choose an AI course?

Use this checklist before you pay for any course, including ours.

| Check | Good sign | Warning sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live or recorded | Live classes where you can ask questions | Only old recordings |
| Your own work | You bring a real project and leave with output | Everyone copies the same demo |
| Proof | The teacher shows real work made with the method | Only screenshots of tools |
| Tools | Current, mainstream tools you can keep using | Tools you have never heard of, or one paid tool only |
| Language | Taught in a language you are comfortable in | Fast English with heavy jargon |
| Recordings | You get the recording of every class | No replay |
| After class | A group or support channel for questions | Nothing after the last session |

Two more questions are worth asking. First, does the course admit what AI cannot do? A trainer who says AI will produce your working drawings or structural design is selling, not teaching. Second, is it built for your field? An architect, an interior designer and a site engineer use the same tools for very different jobs.

## Can you learn AI for architecture for free?

Yes, to a useful level. The tools have free plans, and the method is public.

1. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and paste the project card from our [prompts for architects](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-architects/).
2. Take one live project and run three prompts on it this week: the design brief, the concept options and one client email.
3. Upload one sketch and follow the [sketch to render](/blog/sketch-to-render-ai/) steps.
4. Turn the best image into a clip with the [walkthrough video](/blog/ai-walkthrough-video-from-render/) guide.

:::prompt Make my own AI learning plan | ChatGPT, Gemini

Self-learning works for people who practise every week. A live course mainly buys you speed, feedback on your own work, and other professionals to learn with.

## What can AI not do for an architect?

- It does not produce measured, buildable drawings.
- It does not do structural design or sign anything.
- It does not know the current building rules unless you paste them in.
- It can invent details in images and facts in text, so every output needs your check.

A course that is honest about these limits is worth more than one that promises magic.
