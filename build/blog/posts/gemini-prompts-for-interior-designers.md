:::takeaways
- Start from a real photo of the room. The result stays believable because the walls, windows and light are real.
- Every image prompt needs a keep line: "Keep the walls, windows, doors, ceiling and camera angle exactly the same."
- Change one thing per message: first the style, then the sofa, then the lighting.
- Use real material names and finishes, such as "matt walnut veneer", not "nice wood".
- AI does not know your stock, sizes or prices. Use it to agree on a direction, then specify real products yourself.
:::

## How do you get a good interior design image from Gemini?

Gemini's image model (nicknamed Nano Banana) can edit a photo you upload. Google's own tips for it are simple: say directly what should change, change one thing at a time, and say what must stay the same. For interiors that becomes a four-part prompt:

| Part | What to write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep | The structure that must not move | "Keep walls, windows, doors, ceiling and camera angle exactly the same" |
| Style | One clear direction | "Warm minimal, Japandi influence" |
| Materials | Floors, walls, furniture, fabrics | "Light oak floor, lime-wash walls, linen sofa, cane and teak" |
| Light | Time and fixtures | "Soft daylight, warm cove lighting, one pendant over the dining table" |

Take the photo well and the prompt has less work to do: stand in a corner, hold the phone level at chest height, switch on the lights, and clear loose clutter.

## Which prompts restyle a room from a photo?

:::prompt 1. Restyle the whole room | Gemini, ChatGPT
Redesign this room as a [STYLE, FOR EXAMPLE warm minimal Japandi] interior.

Keep the walls, windows, doors, ceiling height and camera angle exactly the same. Do not move or resize any opening.

Floor: [MATERIAL AND FINISH].
Walls: [COLOUR OR FINISH].
Furniture: [KEY PIECES, FOR EXAMPLE a low linen sofa, a round teak coffee table, a cane armchair].
Soft furnishings: [CURTAINS, RUG, CUSHIONS].
Lighting: [DAYLIGHT / WARM EVENING], with [COVE LIGHT / PENDANT / FLOOR LAMP].

Photorealistic interior photograph, natural colours, no people, no text.
:::

:::prompt 2. Three style options for the same room | Gemini, ChatGPT
Using my room photo, create 3 separate design options so my client can choose a direction. Keep the walls, windows, doors and camera angle exactly the same in all three.

Option 1: [FOR EXAMPLE modern Kerala: teak, cane, white walls, brass details]
Option 2: [FOR EXAMPLE warm minimal: oak, linen, beige and off-white]
Option 3: [FOR EXAMPLE contemporary luxury: marble, dark veneer, soft gold accents]

Same furniture layout in all three, so only the style changes. Photorealistic. Show 3 separate images.
:::

:::prompt 3. Furnish an empty room (virtual staging) | Gemini, ChatGPT
This is a photo of an empty [BEDROOM / LIVING ROOM / OFFICE]. Furnish it for a [FAMILY / YOUNG COUPLE / RENTAL LISTING].

Keep the walls, floor, windows, doors and camera angle exactly the same. Do not change the size of the room.

Add: [LIST, FOR EXAMPLE a queen bed with an upholstered headboard, two side tables, a wardrobe on the left wall, a study desk near the window].
Keep clear walking space of about 3 feet around the bed and in front of the wardrobe.
Style: [STYLE]. Colours: [PALETTE].
Light: soft daylight from the existing window.

Photorealistic, furniture in correct scale for the room, no people, no text.
:::

## Which prompts change only one thing?

Once the client likes a direction, stop regenerating the whole room. Reply in the same chat and change one element. This keeps everything else steady.

:::prompt 4. One-change edits | Gemini, ChatGPT
Use these one at a time, as replies to the image you just got:

- Keep everything exactly the same. Only change the sofa fabric to [COLOUR AND MATERIAL].
- Keep everything exactly the same. Only change the floor to [MATERIAL, SIZE AND FINISH].
- Keep everything exactly the same. Only change the wall behind the TV to [FLUTED PANEL / STONE / TEXTURE PAINT].
- Keep everything exactly the same. Only change the lighting to evening, with warm cove lights and the pendant on.
- Keep everything exactly the same. Remove the [ITEM].
:::

:::prompt 5. Put a real product in the room | Gemini, ChatGPT
Image 1 is my room design. Image 2 is a product photo of a [SOFA / LIGHT / TILE / WALLPAPER].

Place the product from image 2 into the room in image 1, at [POSITION]. Match the room's lighting, shadows and perspective. Keep the product's exact shape, colour and pattern. Keep everything else in the room the same.
:::

## Which prompts build mood boards and material palettes?

:::prompt 6. Mood board image | Gemini, ChatGPT
Create a mood board for a [ROOM OR HOME] in a [STYLE] style.

Layout: a clean flat lay on a [WHITE / LINEN] background, seen from above.
Include: 2 fabric swatches, 1 wood sample, 1 stone or tile sample, 1 metal finish, 1 paint colour card, and 2 small inspiration photos of the space.
Palette: [3 TO 5 COLOURS].

Soft natural light, premium styling, no text or labels.
:::

:::prompt 7. Colour and material palette in words | Gemini, ChatGPT
Suggest a colour and material palette for this project.

Space: [ROOM OR HOME], [SQ FT]
Client: [WHO LIVES THERE AND HOW THEY USE IT]
Light: [BRIGHT / DIM, WHICH DIRECTION THE WINDOWS FACE]
Climate: warm and humid
Style words: [3 WORDS]
Budget level: [ECONOMY / MID / PREMIUM]

Give me a table: surface, material, finish, colour, and why it suits this home. Add one line on maintenance for each. Then give me 2 things to avoid in a humid climate.
:::

## Which prompts help with client work?

Design is half the job. The other half is writing: questions, scope notes and explanations. These prompts are text only, so they work in any AI chat tool.

:::prompt 8. Client questionnaire | Gemini, ChatGPT
Write a short client questionnaire for a new [HOME / OFFICE / CLINIC / CAFE] interior project that I can send on WhatsApp.

Maximum 15 questions. Cover: who uses the space, daily routine, storage needs, likes and dislikes, colours, budget range, timeline, and things they want to keep.

Use simple words. Make most questions quick to answer with a choice or a few words.
:::

:::prompt 9. Room-by-room scope note | Gemini, ChatGPT
Turn my notes into a clear scope of work the client can sign off.

Format: one section per room. In each: what we will do, what is not included, and decisions still needed from the client.

Do not add work I did not mention. Do not give prices.

My notes:
[PASTE YOUR SITE NOTES]
:::

:::prompt 10. Explain the design to the client | Gemini, ChatGPT
Write a short description of this design that I can send with the images.

Design: [STYLE AND 3 MAIN IDEAS]
Client's main wish: [WHAT THEY ASKED FOR]

Under 120 words, warm and simple, no design jargon. Start with how the room will feel, then the 3 ideas, then ask for the one decision I need: [DECISION].
:::

## What are the limits of AI for interior design?

- **Sizes are not reliable.** A sofa that fits in the image may not fit in the room. Check every piece against your measured drawing.
- **It invents products.** The pieces in the image usually do not exist. Match them to real, available items before you promise anything.
- **Small details drift.** Switch plates, handles and patterns can change between images. Google notes that fine details may not come out perfectly.
- **It does not replace drawings.** Carpenters and site teams need your dimensioned drawings, not a picture.
- **Say it is a concept.** Add "AI concept image" when you share it, so the client does not expect an exact match.

:::faq
### Can Gemini redesign my room from a photo?
Yes. Upload a photo of the room in the Gemini app and describe the style, materials and lighting you want. Tell it to keep the walls, windows, doors and camera angle the same, so only the design changes.

### Is Gemini or ChatGPT better for interior design images?
Both can restyle a room photo with these prompts. Google describes Gemini's image model as built for editing one part of an image and leaving the rest unchanged, which suits room photos. Try the same photo and prompt in both and compare the results.

### Is AI virtual staging free?
You can stage rooms on the free plans of Gemini and ChatGPT. Free plans limit how many images you can make each day, and paid plans give more images and stronger image models.

### Can I use AI interior images for clients and marketing?
Yes, as concept images, and say so. Check the tool's current terms for commercial use, and do not present an AI image as a photo of finished work.

### Will AI replace interior designers?
No. AI makes pictures and drafts. It does not measure a site, manage a carpenter, choose real products within a budget or take responsibility for the result. Designers who use AI simply show more options in less time.
