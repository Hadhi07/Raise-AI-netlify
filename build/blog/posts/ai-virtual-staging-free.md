## What is AI virtual staging?

Virtual staging is adding furniture and decor to a photo of an empty room, so buyers and tenants can picture living there. AI makes it fast and free: you upload the photo, describe the furniture, and an image model draws it into the room.

It is useful for anyone with empty spaces to show:

| Who | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Builders and developers | Flats in a new project before any show flat is furnished |
| Real estate agents | Resale and rental listings where the owner has moved out |
| Interior designers | Showing a client how a bare flat could look on handover day |
| Home owners | Renting out a house or a floor without buying furniture first |

AI staging is a picture, not a design. For full interior options on a client's flat, see our [Gemini prompts for interior designers](/blog/gemini-prompts-for-interior-designers/).

## How do you take a photo that stages well?

The AI keeps what it can see. A clear photo gives a believable result; a dark, tilted photo gives a fake-looking one.

- **Shoot from a corner** so two or three walls show. The room looks bigger and the AI understands its shape.
- **Hold the phone level** at chest height. Tilted phones make leaning walls.
- **Use the normal lens, not ultra-wide.** Ultra-wide stretches the edges and the AI copies the distortion.
- **Switch on every light and open the curtains.** Shoot in daylight.
- **Clean first.** Sweep the floor and remove tools, paint tins and packing.
- **One photo per room angle.** Keep the same angle for all your staging versions.

## What should a staging prompt include?

Six parts. Google's own advice for Gemini's image model is the same idea: say directly what to add, say what must stay the same, and change one thing at a time.

| Part | What to write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep | The room itself | "Keep the walls, floor, ceiling, windows, doors and camera angle exactly the same" |
| Room and buyer | What the room is, who it is for | "Master bedroom in a 2BHK flat for a young couple" |
| Furniture | A short list | "Queen bed, two side tables, a wardrobe on the left wall" |
| Style and colours | One direction | "Warm modern, oak and off-white, sage green accents" |
| Light | Match the photo | "Soft daylight from the existing window" |
| Rules | Scale and space | "Furniture in correct scale, about 3 feet of walking space" |

## Which prompts stage each room?

:::prompt 1. Living room | Gemini, ChatGPT
This is a photo of an empty living room in a [2BHK / 3BHK] flat. Furnish it for [WHO, FOR EXAMPLE a young family].

Keep the walls, floor, ceiling, windows, doors, switches and camera angle exactly the same. Do not change the size of the room or move any opening.

Add: an L-shaped sofa against the [LEFT / BACK] wall, a low coffee table, a TV unit on the [WALL], a rug, two cushions, a floor lamp, one large plant and simple art above the sofa.
Style: [FOR EXAMPLE warm modern, light oak, beige and white, a few green accents].
Light: soft daylight from the existing windows, matching the photo.
Rules: furniture in correct real-world scale for this room, clear walking path from the door to the balcony.

Photorealistic real estate photograph, no people, no text, no watermark.

## How do you stage one room for different buyers?

Different buyers want different things from the same flat. Show the same room three ways, in one go.

:::prompt 5. Same room, three buyers | Gemini, ChatGPT
Using this empty room photo, create 3 staged versions. Keep the room, camera angle, walls, floor, windows and light exactly the same in all three. Change only the furniture and decor:

1. Young working couple: modern, minimal, a work desk by the window.
2. Family with children: a larger sofa, a play corner with a soft rug and storage baskets.
3. Rental tenant: simple, durable, budget-friendly furniture, neutral colours.

Correct scale in all three. Photorealistic, no people, no text. Show 3 separate images.
:::

:::prompt 6. Clear old furniture, then stage | Gemini, ChatGPT
This room still has old furniture and clutter in it.

Step 1: remove all furniture, boxes and loose items, and show the empty room. Keep the walls, floor, windows, doors, switches and camera angle exactly the same. Do not hide or repair any wall marks, cracks or damp patches.

Step 2: once I approve the empty room, I will ask you to furnish it.
:::

The line about not hiding marks matters. Clearing furniture is fine. Hiding damage the buyer would see on a visit is not.

## How do you fix a staged photo that looks wrong?

Reply in the same chat and change one thing. Small requests keep the rest of the image steady.

:::prompt 7. One-change fixes | Gemini, ChatGPT
Use these one at a time, as replies to the staged image:

- Keep everything exactly the same. The sofa is too big for the room. Make it a 3-seater in correct scale.
- Keep everything exactly the same. Remove the rug.
- Keep everything exactly the same. Move the dining table away from the window so the window is not blocked.
- Keep everything exactly the same. The window on the right changed shape. Restore it to match the original photo.
- Keep everything exactly the same. Make the lighting match the original photo: softer, no strong shadows.
:::

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Furniture looks too big or floats above the floor | Ask for "correct real-world scale" and "furniture standing on the floor with soft contact shadows" |
| A window, door or switch moved | Repeat the keep line and ask to restore it to the original photo |
| The room looks bigger than it is | Say "do not change the size or shape of the room", and reject the image if it still does |
| It looks like a painting | Add "photorealistic real estate photograph, natural colours" |
| Too much decor | List only the pieces you want and add "keep it simple, not cluttered" |

## How do you use staged photos honestly?

A staged photo should help a buyer imagine the space, not trick them about the space. Guidance from property listing services such as US MLS boards is consistent, and it is a good standard to follow anywhere:

- **Label every staged photo** on the image itself: "Virtually staged" or "Furniture digitally added". A note at the bottom of the listing is not enough, because people scroll photos and skip text.
- **Show the real empty photo next to it.**
- **Change only movable things.** Furniture, decor and lighting are fine. Walls, floors, windows, views, room size, cracks and damp are not.

| Fine to add with AI | Not fine to change |
| --- | --- |
| Furniture, rugs, curtains, plants, art | Room size or shape |
| Lamps and warm lighting | The view from the window or balcony |
| Removing your old furniture and clutter | Cracks, damp, stains or damage |
| Showing different styles for different buyers | Flooring, fittings or finishes that are not really there |

In India, advertising rules ask that ads do not mislead buyers, and property ads may also fall under your state's RERA rules. Clear labelling keeps you on the right side of both and protects your reputation. Check with your advisor if you are unsure about a specific ad.

:::prompt 8. Honest listing caption | ChatGPT, Gemini
Write a short property listing caption for WhatsApp and Instagram.

Property: [FOR EXAMPLE 2BHK flat, 1,050 sq ft, 3rd floor, east facing, LOCATION]
Key features: [LIST]
Price and contact: [DETAILS OR "DM for price"]

Rules:
- Say clearly that some photos are virtually staged: "Some photos are virtually staged to show how the flat can be furnished. Empty photos are included."
- Do not exaggerate. No words like "best", "luxury" or "guaranteed" unless I give a reason.
- Under 80 words, simple English, end with one call to action.
:::

## What are the limits of AI virtual staging?

- **Scale is approximate.** The AI does not know the room's real size. Check that a sofa in the image would actually fit.
- **The room can drift.** Windows, switches and skirting can change between versions. Compare every image with the original photo.
- **The furniture is imaginary.** Buyers cannot buy the exact pieces they see.
- **Small text fails.** Keep logos and prices out of the image and add them in your own software.
- **Free plans have daily limits.** Plan your rooms and prompts before you start.

:::faq
### Is AI virtual staging really free?
Yes. Gemini and ChatGPT can both stage an uploaded room photo on their free plans. Free plans limit how many images and uploads you get each day, and paid plans give more.

### Which AI is best for virtual staging?
Both Gemini and ChatGPT work with the prompts in this guide. Gemini's image model is designed to edit an uploaded image while keeping the rest of it unchanged, which suits staging a real room. Try the same photo and prompt in both and keep the better result.

### Can I use virtually staged photos in property ads in India?
Yes, if you are honest about it. Label each staged photo "Virtually staged", show the empty photo beside it, and never change the room itself. Ads must not mislead buyers, and property ads may also need to follow your state's RERA rules, so check with your advisor for a specific project.

### Can I stage a room from a phone photo?
Yes. A clear phone photo taken from a corner, held level, in daylight with the lights on, works well. Use the normal camera lens rather than ultra-wide.

### Will virtual staging replace a real show flat?
No. It helps buyers picture the space online and brings more of them to visit. A real visit still decides the sale, so the flat has to match what the photos honestly show.
