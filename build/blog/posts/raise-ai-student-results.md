## How do you know if an AI course actually works?

You look at what its students made after the course. Every institute says it is the best. Only results can be checked.

A good AI course leaves you with finished work you can use in your job or sell to a client. So the fair test is simple: can the institute show you its students, their work and their own words? If it can only show a certificate, a syllabus or a famous tool logo, you are being sold a promise.

| What they show you | What it proves | How much to trust it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certificate sample | You attended | Low |
| Syllabus and tool list | What they plan to teach | Low |
| Screenshots of AI tools | The tools exist | Low |
| Named students with their profession | Real people took it | Medium |
| Finished work by students that you can watch | Students can actually do it | High |
| Reviews in the students' own voices | Students were happy enough to say so | High |
| Work made for paying clients | The skill earns money | Highest |

Because every class is built to end with something finished, not with notes. Raise AI teaches in three steps:

1. **Learn it.** Every class is live. The method is shown step by step, with the exact prompts.
2. **Build it.** In the next class, you build the same thing yourself, live. Your work is reviewed and anything stuck is fixed.
3. **Use it at work.** Every lesson is tied to your profession, so what you build is something you can use or sell.

Classes are live on Google Meet, taught in simple Malayalam with standard English technical terms, and you get the recording after each class.

## How do you check any AI institute in Kerala?

Use these questions before you pay anyone, including us. Copy the prompt to compare two or three courses side by side.

:::prompt Compare AI courses before you join | ChatGPT, Gemini
I want to join an AI course. Help me compare these options fairly.

My profession: [YOUR JOB]
What I want to be able to do after the course: [GOAL]
My budget and time: [RS RANGE], [HOURS PER WEEK]

Course options (paste what each website says):
1. [COURSE 1 NAME AND DETAILS]
2. [COURSE 2 NAME AND DETAILS]
3. [COURSE 3 NAME AND DETAILS]

Make a table that scores each course on: live teaching, work I will finish, named students, student work I can watch, reviews in students' own voices, recordings, support after the course, and price.

Then list the 5 questions I should ask each institute on WhatsApp before paying. Do not assume anything that is not in the text I pasted.
:::

The questions that matter most:

- Can I see work made by your students, not by the trainer?
- Can I see or hear reviews from named students?
- What will I have finished by the last class?
- Is it live, and do I get the recordings?
- Is it taught in a language I am comfortable with?
- What is the refund policy if it is not what you described?
