:::takeaways
- You can start from a photo of an existing house, a floor plan with the plot width, a hand sketch or a model screenshot.
- The prompt formula is Keep + Building facts + Style + Materials + Light and camera + Context.
- "Keep the door and window positions" is the most important line. Without it, the AI moves openings and the design stops matching the plan.
- Show the client three styles on the same house, then refine one, changing a single thing per message.
- An AI elevation is a picture, not a drawing. It has no scale, and a contractor cannot build from it.
:::

## What is AI front elevation design?

It is using an AI image tool to show how the front of a house could look before anyone draws it in detail. You give the AI the facts of the house and the style you want, and it returns a realistic picture of the front facade.

In India, "front elevation design" usually means the street-facing look of the house: the roof, the openings, the balcony, the cladding, the compound wall and the gate. That is exactly where AI is useful, because clients decide on looks, and looks are slow to show by hand.

| What AI is good for | What AI cannot do |
| --- | --- |
| Showing 3 to 5 style options in one sitting | Give you a scaled, dimensioned elevation drawing |
| A facelift idea on a photo of an old house | Check setbacks, height limits or structure |
| Trying materials: stone, HPL, wood, tile, paint | Tell you what a material costs or how it weathers on your site |
| Day and night views for a client presentation | Promise the built house will match the picture |

## What do you need to start?

Only one of these, plus Gemini or ChatGPT on a phone or laptop:

| Starting point | Best for | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo of an existing house | Renovation and facelift jobs | Stand across the road, phone level, whole facade in frame |
| Floor plan and plot width | New houses at concept stage | Give the plot width, floors and which side faces the road |
| Hand sketch of the elevation | Testing your own idea quickly | Darken the outline and label glass, roof and balcony |
| Model screenshot (SketchUp, Revit) | The most faithful result | Plain white model, camera already where you want it |

For new houses, a quick massing sketch or a clay model screenshot gives far better results than a floor plan alone, because the AI no longer has to guess heights. Our [sketch to render guide](/blog/sketch-to-render-ai/) covers that route in detail.

## What should an elevation prompt include?

Six parts, in this order. Google's own tips for Gemini's image model point the same way: say directly what to change, say what must stay the same, and edit in small steps.

| Part | What to write | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep | What must not move | "Keep the door and window positions, roof line and number of floors" |
| Building facts | Floors, width, road side | "G+1 house on a 30 ft wide plot, road on the east side" |
| Style | One clear direction | "Contemporary box type" or "Kerala style with sloping tile roof" |
| Materials | Each surface | "Grey textured paint, one stone-clad box, teak HPL panel at the entrance" |
| Light and camera | Time and viewpoint | "Late afternoon sun, eye-level view from across the road" |
| Context | What is around it | "Compound wall with sliding gate, small lawn, neighbouring houses" |

## Which prompts work from a photo, a plan or a sketch?

:::prompt 1. Facelift from a photo of an existing house | Gemini, ChatGPT
This is a photo of an existing house. Give it a new front elevation design.

Keep: the number of floors, the roof line, and every door and window in the same position and size. Do not add or remove floors or openings. Keep the camera angle.
New style: [FOR EXAMPLE contemporary with clean lines].
Materials: [FOR EXAMPLE light grey textured paint, charcoal frame around the first floor windows, stone cladding on the ground floor wall beside the entrance, wooden HPL panel above the main door].
Add: [FOR EXAMPLE a slim concrete canopy over the entrance, modern railing on the balcony].
Compound wall and gate: [STYLE].
Light: soft late afternoon sunlight.

Photorealistic architectural photograph, natural colours, no people, no cars, no text.
:::

:::prompt 2. New elevation from a floor plan | Gemini, ChatGPT
This is the ground floor plan of a new house. Create a photorealistic front elevation view of it.

House facts:
- Plot width: [FOR EXAMPLE 30 ft]. Road on the [EAST] side.
- Floors: [G+1]. Roof: [FLAT / SLOPED / MIXED].
- Front of the house has: [FOR EXAMPLE main door on the left, living room window in the centre, car porch on the right, balcony above the porch].

Keep the door, window and porch positions as shown on the plan. Do not add extra floors.
Style: [STYLE]. Materials: [MATERIALS].
Camera: eye level, straight-on view from across the road, the whole house and compound wall in frame.
Context: [NEIGHBOURING HOUSES, TREES, STREET].

Photorealistic, no people, no text.
:::

:::prompt 3. Elevation from my hand sketch | Gemini, ChatGPT
Turn this hand sketch of a house front into a photorealistic elevation image.

Keep exactly what the sketch shows: the shape, the roof, the number of floors and the position of every door, window and balcony. Read the labels I wrote on the sketch.
Materials: [LIST BY PART OF THE HOUSE].
Light: [MORNING / LATE AFTERNOON / OVERCAST MONSOON SKY].
Context: [COMPOUND WALL, GATE, PLANTS, ROAD].

Real photograph style, straight vertical lines, no people, no text.
:::

## Which prompts give you different elevation styles?

Run these on the same house image, so the client compares styles and not different buildings.

:::prompt 4. Contemporary box type elevation | Gemini, ChatGPT
Redesign the front of this house as a contemporary box type elevation.

Keep the floors, openings and roof line exactly as they are.
Style: clean geometric boxes, flat surfaces, very little ornament. One box frame around the first floor projected forward and finished in [CHARCOAL / STONE CLADDING / WOOD-LOOK HPL], the rest in [WHITE / LIGHT GREY] textured paint.
Details: thin horizontal grooves on the plain walls, slim glass railing on the balcony, a vertical strip of louvres beside the staircase window.
Night touch: warm concealed lights under the box frame.

Photorealistic, late afternoon light, no people, no text.
:::

:::prompt 5. Kerala style elevation (traditional or contemporary) | Gemini, ChatGPT
Redesign the front of this house in a [TRADITIONAL / CONTEMPORARY] Kerala style.

Keep the number of floors and the position of every door and window.
Roof: sloping roof with terracotta clay tiles and deep overhangs to shed heavy monsoon rain.
Walls: [WHITE LIME PLASTER / EXPOSED LATERITE / MIX].
Details: [FOR EXAMPLE a front sit-out with round pillars, wooden charupadi seating, teak wood windows with wooden frames, a small gable at the entrance].
Context: coconut palms, a laterite compound wall, a gravel courtyard.
Light: soft morning light after rain.

Photorealistic architectural photograph, no people, no text.
:::

:::prompt 6. Tropical modern elevation for a rainy, hot climate | Gemini, ChatGPT
Redesign the front of this house as a tropical modern elevation suited to heavy rain and strong sun.

Keep the floors and openings exactly as they are.
Add: deep concrete sunshades over the windows, vertical wooden louvres on the [WEST] facing side, a sloped metal or tile roof over the terrace, planter boxes on the balcony.
Materials: off-white textured paint, warm wood-look panels, grey stone at the base.
Context: lush garden, frangipani tree, wet paving after rain.

Photorealistic, overcast monsoon sky with soft light, no people, no text.
:::

:::prompt 7. Three options for the client | Gemini, ChatGPT
Using this house image, create 3 front elevation options for my client to compare.

Keep the house, floors, openings, camera angle and surroundings exactly the same in all three. Change only the style and materials:

Option A: contemporary box type, grey and charcoal with one stone-clad box.
Option B: contemporary Kerala, white walls and a sloping clay tile roof.
Option C: tropical modern, wood louvres and deep sunshades.

Show 3 separate images in the same light and time of day.
:::

## How do you refine the chosen elevation?

Once the client picks a direction, stop regenerating the whole house. Reply in the same chat and change one thing at a time. The AI keeps the rest steady when the request is small.

:::prompt 8. Refine and present: one-change edits and a night view | Gemini, ChatGPT
Use these one at a time, as replies to the elevation you chose:

- Keep everything exactly the same. Only change the stone cladding to [COLOUR AND TYPE].
- Keep everything exactly the same. Only change the main gate to a [SLIDING MS GATE WITH WOOD-LOOK PANELS].
- Keep everything exactly the same. Only make the balcony railing [GLASS / MS FLAT BARS / WOODEN].
- Keep everything exactly the same. Only add a small name plate and a lamp beside the gate.
- Keep the house exactly the same. Show it at night: warm interior lights on, soft wall-washer lights on the front wall, a light above the gate, a deep blue sky.
:::

## Which elevation style suits which house?

Use this as a talking point with the client, not a rule. Climate, plot, budget and the street all change the answer.

| Style | What it looks like | Works well when | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contemporary box type | Flat roof, clean boxes, one feature material | Narrow city plots, G+1 and G+2 houses | Plain light walls show rain stains, so plan drip edges and sunshades |
| Kerala traditional | Sloping clay tile roof, sit-out, wood details | Wider plots, families who want a classic look | Roof slopes and overhangs need space and careful detailing |
| Contemporary Kerala | Sloping roof mixed with flat modern parts | Clients who want both modern and familiar | Too many roof types on one facade can look busy |
| Tropical modern | Louvres, deep shades, planters, wood tones | Hot, rainy places and west-facing fronts | Wood-look and planters need upkeep |

## What are the limits of AI elevations?

- **No scale.** Heights, sill levels and proportions in the image are approximate. Never take a dimension from it.
- **Openings can drift.** Even with the keep line, check every door and window against your plan before showing the client.
- **Rules are not checked.** Setbacks, height limits and building rules are not part of the picture. Check them in your local rules as usual.
- **Materials are guesses.** The AI shows a look, not a real product. Match it to cladding, tiles and paint that are actually available.
- **Fine text and logos fail.** Add the house name later in your own software.
- **Call it a concept.** A line such as "AI concept image, not the final elevation" avoids arguments later.

:::faq
### Can AI design a house front elevation for free?
Yes, for concept images. Gemini and ChatGPT both accept a photo, plan or sketch and return an elevation image on their free plans, with daily limits on images and uploads. The final elevation drawing still needs an architect or engineer.

### Which AI is best for house elevation design?
Gemini and ChatGPT both work with the prompts in this guide. Gemini's image model is designed to edit one part of an uploaded image and leave the rest unchanged, which helps when you restyle a photo of a real house. Try the same image and prompt in both and keep the better result.

### Can AI make a front elevation from my floor plan?
It can create a realistic front view based on the plan, if you tell it the plot width, the number of floors and where the doors, windows and porch are. Because a plan has no heights, the result is an interpretation. A simple massing sketch or model screenshot gives a more accurate picture.

### Can my contractor build from an AI elevation image?
No. The image has no dimensions, levels or details. Use it to agree the look with the client, then prepare the proper elevation and detail drawings for the site.

### Can I use these prompts for a Kerala style house?
Yes. Prompt 5 is written for Kerala homes, with sloping clay tile roofs, deep overhangs, sit-outs and laterite. Add the details your client wants, such as a nadumuttam or charupadi, in plain words.
:::
