About us

Everyone was worrying. So we started Raise AI.

Raise AI is an AI upskilling institute in Kerala, India. We teach working professionals in every field, from doctors and lawyers to architects and editors, how to use AI in their own job.

It started with a funny video.

Hadhi posted a video of his mom teaching him about AI. She did not know he already worked with AI every day. A lot of people watched it.

The comments were not funny.

Many people wrote the same thing: AI is going to take my job.

His answer was a bicycle.

A runner is fast. A runner with a bicycle is faster. AI is the bicycle. Jobs that repeat one simple task will go. But people who worked hard to learn a real skill get faster with AI.

So he started Raise AI, to teach people how to ride.
