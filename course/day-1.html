Day 1

Introduction to Editing in Flow

Lesson Overview

In this lesson, we dive into AI video generation and Timeline Creation. You will learn the best practices for shooting source video (e.g., shooting in plain spaces without prominent characters for better AI outputs) and the workflow for replacing or generating new elements in your timeline. We cover how to use tools like Meta AI to generate images, specify exact timestamps, and seamlessly integrate them into your project. Make sure to complete the assignment in the group, and note down any questions and send them to us on WhatsApp.

Key Takeaways:

  • How to shoot source video that works well with AI (plain spaces, no prominent characters)
  • Replacing or generating new elements in your timeline
  • Generating images with Meta AI and placing them at exact timestamps
