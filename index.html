Knowledge & FAQ

Everything you need to know about learning AI.

How can working professionals in Kerala learn and use AI in their careers?

Working professionals across diverse fields—including doctors, lawyers, architects, designers, editors, and educators—can learn practical AI applications through live online interactive classes. You learn how to use generative AI, custom scriptwriting GPTs, and automated workflows directly in your everyday tasks without requiring coding knowledge or prior technical backgrounds.

Can I create professional AI video ads without filming equipment or editing experience?

Yes. With modern generative tools like Google Flow, Luma Dream Machine, ElevenLabs, and CapCut, you can produce complete 30-second commercial advertisements entirely on your laptop. No camera, studio, lighting equipment, or actors are required to get started.

What are the best free AI video generators for commercial ads in 2026?

The leading free-tier AI video tools include Google Flow, Luma Dream Machine, Kling AI, and Hailuo. These platforms allow creators to generate photorealistic b-roll, commercial product visuals, and character animations directly in standard web browsers.

Will AI tools replace video editors and graphic designers in Kerala?

AI will not replace skilled creators; creators who leverage AI will replace those who do not. AI handles tedious repetitive rendering, masking, and b-roll sourcing, enabling editors and designers to finish client deliverables 5x faster and charge premium rates.

Are the live AI classes conducted in Malayalam or English?

Classes are conducted live in simple Malayalam with standard industry English technical terms. This ensures that every concept is completely clear and comfortable for learners across Kerala.

How can business owners in Kerala use AI to automate operations and cut costs?

Business owners use AI to automate customer support on WhatsApp, generate localized video commercials in minutes, and streamline internal reporting. We offer tailored 1-to-1 private strategy sessions with founder Hadhi Muhammed P. to audit your business and design custom AI workflows.

What is Google Flow and how does generative AI video work?

Google Flow is an advanced generative video creation workflow that transforms descriptive text prompts and reference images into fluid cinematic video scenes. It empowers creators to simulate camera movement, lighting, and product physics effortlessly.

What certificate and portfolio do students finish with?

Students who complete the AI Ad Video Production Masterclass finish with 7 production-grade commercial portfolio pieces—including swapped hero assets, UGC ads, and character consistency reels—along with a verified Certificate of Completion.

Do I need a high-end computer or paid software to start learning?

No. The live beginner workshop and courses operate entirely on cloud-based browser tools. A standard laptop with a stable internet connection is all you need.

How do I choose between the 2-hour AI workshop and the 14-day masterclass?

If you are new to AI video tools, begin with the 2-hour Live AI Workshop to create your first completed video using free tools. If you are an active creator or editor seeking client-grade commercial production skills, join the 14-day masterclass.
