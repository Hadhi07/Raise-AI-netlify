Knowledge & FAQ

Everything you need to know about learning AI.

What is Raise AI?

Raise AI is an AI upskilling institute in Kerala, India. We teach working professionals in every field, from doctors and lawyers to architects and editors, how to use AI tools in their own job, in live online classes.

Is this an online AI course?

Yes. Every AI class is online and live on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere. You also get the recording after each class.

What is the best AI course for a beginner?

The best AI course is one where you build real work for your own job, not one where you only watch. If you are new, start with our 2-hour live AI workshop. It uses free tools and needs no experience.

Who are these AI classes for?

Working professionals who already have a skill and want to learn about AI to do it faster: doctors, lawyers, architects, teachers, designers, video editors and creators. You do not need any coding.

Which AI tools will I learn?

We teach the latest AI tools that are useful for real work, such as Google Flow, Veo, Gemini, ChatGPT, ElevenLabs and Meta AI. We update the classes when better tools come out.

Do I need paid tools or a powerful computer?

No. The live AI workshop uses only free tools that run in your browser. In the main course we set up the tools together with you.

I run a business. Is there a course for me?

Every business needs AI in a different way, so we do not run a fixed course for business owners. Book a 1-to-1 session with Hadhi on WhatsApp and we plan it around your business.

Are the live AI classes conducted in Malayalam or English?

Classes are conducted live in simple Malayalam with standard industry English technical terms. This ensures that every concept is completely clear and comfortable for learners across Kerala.

Do I get a certificate after the AI course?

Yes. Students who finish the AI Ad Video Production Masterclass get a Certificate of Completion.

What is the refund policy?

If the class does not match what we described, you can ask for a refund within 3 days of paying. Approved refunds reach you in 5 to 7 working days. Read the full refund policy.

How do I contact Raise AI?

WhatsApp is fastest: +91 73560 03301, every day from 9 AM to 10 PM. You can also email support@raiseai.in.
